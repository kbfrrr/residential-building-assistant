# Residential Building Resident Handbook

Version: 1.0 draft

All building details, contacts, notices, and policies in this handbook are invented for the SAM10 school project. Residential Building is a placeholder name.

## 1. Building office

The building office is on the ground floor. Office hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. The fictional office email is office@residential.example.com. Routine inquiries received outside office hours are handled on the next office day. The security desk operates 24 hours a day.

## 2. Amenities

The gym is open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. The swimming pool is open Tuesday to Sunday from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM and closed every Monday for cleaning. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult in the pool area. Glass containers are prohibited around the pool.

The function room can be used from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Residents must request it through the building office at least three days in advance. Requests must include the date, start and end times, and expected number of guests. Capacity is 20 people. The office confirms availability and any applicable charges. This handbook does not list a rental fee.

## 3. Maintenance reporting

Report routine maintenance concerns to the building office by email or in person. Describe the issue, its location, when it began, and a preferred contact method. In a real service, photographs may help, but use invented examples only in this school demo. The office acknowledges routine reports within one office day. Acknowledgment is not a guaranteed repair completion time.

For an immediate hazard, contact the security desk directly. The assistant does not monitor emergencies or submit maintenance reports. It must not provide hazardous electrical, gas, or structural repair instructions.

## 4. Visitors and deliveries

Visitors register at the security desk. Security contacts the resident before allowing entry. Visitors may use shared amenities only when accompanied by a resident and subject to the amenity rules. Contractors and moving crews follow the separate moving or work-approval procedures.

Couriers leave ordinary parcels at the ground-floor parcel desk. Collection hours are daily from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Residents should collect parcels within 48 hours of notification. Food deliveries are collected by the resident in the lobby. The parcel desk does not accept refrigerated goods or cash-on-delivery payments.

## 5. Parking

Residents may park only in their assigned spaces. Visitor spaces require security approval and are available from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, subject to availability. Overnight visitor parking requires advance approval from the building office. Driveways, emergency access points, and another resident's assigned space must remain clear. This handbook does not specify parking penalties or replacement-sticker fees.

## 6. Moving in and out

Moving hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Moving is prohibited on Sundays and public holidays. Request approval and a service-elevator slot from the building office at least two days in advance. Include the unit number, moving date, preferred two-hour slot, and moving-company name if applicable. The office confirms the slot before the move. Furniture and large boxes use the service elevator. Moving crews register at security before starting.

The assistant explains this procedure but cannot reserve a slot or confirm availability. This handbook does not specify a moving deposit or approval documents beyond the request details above.

## 7. House rules

Quiet hours are daily from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Keep noise low enough that it does not disturb neighboring units. Renovation work requires building-office approval and is permitted Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, excluding public holidays.

Pets must be registered with the building office. Keep pets leashed in shared areas, clean up after them, and keep them outside the pool and gym. The handbook does not specify breed, weight, or pet-count limits. Ask the office about cases the handbook does not cover.

## 8. Waste and recycling

The ground-floor waste room is open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Separate general waste from recyclables. Place clean paper, flattened cardboard, and empty recyclable containers in the labeled bins. Do not leave waste in corridors or stairwells. Arrange bulky-item disposal through the building office at least two days in advance. Batteries, electronics, and hazardous materials require office guidance and must not go in ordinary bins.

## 9. Assistant boundaries

The assistant provides information from this handbook. It cannot submit reports, approve requests, reserve amenities, accept payments, check live availability, or disclose resident identities or contact details. For missing information, it should say that the handbook does not specify it and refer the user to the building office. It must not invent prices, approvals, exceptions, or completion dates.

Only invented demonstration information may be entered into this school system. Do not enter real resident names, unit assignments, contact details, or personal records. Handbook examples are not evidence that a live operation or booking was performed.
